## REFERENCE
The reps view includes a review of the actions they have scheduled or are due today (meetings, calls, emails, follow ups, etc). View also includes updates or new opportunities, that include next action, next phase changes, opportunity value. Reps can update these values. Reps validate or reject any suggested updates.

## CONSTRAINTS
Updates are pulled as is (no summarization) from their sources.  Every update must reflect a formative change (value, status, next action). Status can only be the five major phases (New, Discovery, Qualify, Negotiate, Closed). Opportunity values must be stated numbers, not an AI estimate or calculation.

## OUTPUT
Produce a fully clickable and functional webpage for reps, with review, update, and validate processes. Responsive and ready to share via link
