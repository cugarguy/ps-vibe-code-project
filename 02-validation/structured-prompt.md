## REFERENCE
The reps view includes a review of the actions they have scheduled or are due today (meetings, calls, emails, follow ups, etc). View also includes updates or new opportunities, that include next action, phase changes, opportunity value. Reps can validate or reject any suggested updates, or they can update these values and save the changes. Saving changes validates the update.

## CONSTRAINTS
Source quotes are pulled verbatim (no summarization) .  Every update must reflect a change to at least one of value, phase, next action. Phases must be one of the five major phases (New, Discovery, Qualify, Negotiate, Closed). Opportunity values must appear in the source; values should not be AI estimates or calculations.
